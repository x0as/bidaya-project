What is Trebuchet?

Trebuchet is Bidaya's premier project launch and incubation program—where bold ideas are transformed into impactful realities. Whether you're dreaming of building a startup, conducting groundbreaking research, or creating something revolutionary, Trebuchet provides the momentum you need.

Just as a trebuchet launches projectiles to their destination, we launch ambitious student innovators toward their goals. With dedicated mentorship, access to resources, and a network of builders and investors, your vision becomes unstoppable.
